# ERA 12-Week Personalized Program

Welcome to your 12-week training program. This plan is built around 6 training days plus one active recovery day each week, progressing through three distinct phases designed to build muscle, develop strength, and peak your performance.

- **12 weeks** of structured progression
- **6 training days + 1 rest/walk day** per week
- **3 phases**: Hypertrophy (W1-W4), Strength (W5-W8), Peak (W9-W12)

## Weekly Schedule

Your training week stays the same every week — only the load and intensity progress.

| Day | Workout | Duration |

## Phase Summary

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Hypertrophy | W1-W4 | Muscle building, moderate weight, 10-12 reps |
| Strength | W5-W8 | Heavier loads, lower reps |
| Peak | W9-W12 | Max effort, peak weights, PR attempts |

## How to Use This Plan

Train Monday through Saturday and take Sunday as an active recovery day with an outdoor walk. Log every set in the ERA app as you go — your weights and reps build the data that drives the program.

The weights listed for each week are starting targets. The app will automatically adjust your prescribed loads based on how your sessions actually go, so trust the process and push for quality reps over ego lifting. PR attempts come in the Peak phase (W9-W12) — fuel and sleep well in those weeks.
